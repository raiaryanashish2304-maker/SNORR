SAMPLE LESSON
✳　THE BIG IDEA

A leaf is a tiny solar-powered kitchen: it turns light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen.

A QUICK WALK-THROUGH
01
Catch the light

Chlorophyll inside the leaf absorbs sunlight. Think of it as the energy switch that starts the process.

02
Bring in the ingredients

Water travels up from the roots. Carbon dioxide slips in through tiny pores on the leaf.

03
Make and share the food

The leaf uses that energy to make glucose, a sugar the plant can use. Oxygen is released back into the air.

KEEP THIS IN YOUR POCKETLight + water + carbon dioxide → glucose + oxygen
